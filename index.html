Case study

OrbitCV

Problem

Building a good resume still takes too long for many job seekers because the process is usually split across messy editors, templates, and manual formatting work.

My role

I founded and built OrbitCV end to end — frontend, auth, CRUD flows, AI integration, payments, database setup, deployment, and product iteration.

Tech + trade-offs

React and Supabase were the right choice because I needed to move fast with a real product while keeping reliable auth and data storage. TanStack Query became important once I traced early UI slowness to caching and data fetching. The trade-off was extra state and integration complexity, but it let me ship faster and keep improving a live product.

Measurable outcome

  • Around 15–20 monthly users.
  • 25+ resumes generated.
  • 1 paid user, which validated real willingness to pay.
  • Roughly 90 minutes saved per resume session compared with building from scratch.

What I learned

Performance issues are often data-flow issues in disguise. Once I improved the fetch and cache strategy, the product felt faster immediately. I also learned how much confidence better validation and clearer UX creates in a product tied to job applications.

Case study

Ignited Youth App

Problem

Youth communities often depend on scattered messages and inconsistent reminders. I wanted a product that gives young Christians steadier prompts and a clearer way to stay engaged.

My role

I built the entire app myself, including the UI, authentication, data layer, and notification flow.

Tech + trade-offs

A web app was the fastest way to launch something accessible on any device. React, Supabase, and Resend covered the core needs without unnecessary overhead. The trade-off is that the product is still in its rollout stage while the domain is being finalized, so usage metrics aren't the story yet.

Measurable outcome

  • Built a working MVP with auth, email notifications, and a database-backed content flow.
  • Shipped the entire product as a solo builder.
  • Current focus is launch readiness and a smoother rollout path.

What I learned

Reminder products need more than notifications. The habit only sticks when the experience feels clear, calm, and easy to return to.

Case study

Axion Outreach

Problem

Prospecting was scattered across notes, browser tabs, and half-written pitch drafts. I needed one tool that kept the workflow tight.

My role

I designed and built the entire product for my own workflow, which meant I could iterate quickly and make trade-offs based on actual daily use.

Tech + trade-offs

React, Supabase, and the OpenAI API let me manage prospect data and generate client pitches from one interface. Because the tool was built for me first, I optimized for speed and usefulness over broad customization — faster iteration, even with limited external feedback.

Measurable outcome

  • Built a single interface for prospect tracking and pitch generation.
  • Reduced the need to manage outreach across scattered tools.
  • Usage is internal, so outcomes are practical but not formally tracked yet.

What I learned

Internal tools are one of the fastest ways to sharpen product instincts. You feel immediately whether the flow helps you move faster or adds friction.
