Case study

OrbitCV

Problem

Building a good resume still takes too long for many job seekers because the process is usually split across messy editors, templates, and manual formatting work.

My role

I founded and built OrbitCV end to end. I owned the frontend, auth, CRUD flows, AI integration, payments, database setup, deployment, and product iteration.

Tech + trade-offs

React and Supabase were the right choice because I needed to move fast with a real product and still have reliable auth and data storage. TanStack Query became important once I identified caching and data fetching as the real source of early UI slowness. The trade-off was extra state and integration complexity, but it let me ship faster and keep improving a live product.

Measurable outcome

  • Around 15–20 monthly users.
  • 25+ resumes generated.
  • 1 paid user, which validated real willingness to pay.
  • Roughly 90 minutes saved per resume session compared with building from scratch.

What I learned

Performance issues are often data-flow issues in disguise. Once I improved the fetch and cache strategy, the product felt faster immediately. I also learned how much confidence better validation and clearer UX can create in a product tied to job applications.

My role

Measurable outcome

What I learned

Reminder products need more than notifications. The habit only sticks when the experience feels clear, calm, and easy to return to.

Problem

Prospecting was scattered across notes, browser tabs, and half-written pitch drafts. I needed one tool that kept the workflow tight.

My role

I designed and built the entire product for my own workflow, which meant I could iterate quickly and make trade-offs based on actual daily use.

Measurable outcome

  • Reduced the need to manage outreach across scattered tools.
  • Usage is internal, so outcomes are practical but not formally tracked yet.

What I learned

Internal tools are one of the fastest ways to sharpen product instincts. You immediately feel whether the flow helps you move faster or adds friction.
